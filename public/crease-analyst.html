How to use the player
  1. Tap Tools to show or hide these controls. Load video 1, then press Play. Tap the picture to play or pause.
  2. Frame buttons move one frame. Speed gives slow motion.
  3. Zoom with two fingers: pinch out to zoom in, pinch in to zoom out, even while the video plays. Drag with one finger to move around. The 1× button resets it.
  4. Screenshot saves what you see. In Side by side it saves both videos in one picture.
  5. Side by side shows two videos. Load video 2, line each video up at the same moment (for example ball release), then choose Both together so play, pause and frame steps move them in step.
  6. Overlay puts video 2 on top of video 1. Use See-through to fade between them.
  7. Draw: Line, Arrow, Circle and Pen draw with your finger. Angle: tap three points and the middle one shows the angle.
  8. Repeat: set a start and an end, then turn Repeat on to watch one action again and again.
  9. Ball speed: stop on the frame of release and mark it, stop on the frame the ball reaches the batter and mark it. The speed is an average and only an estimate.

Save your data

Your tags are kept in this browser on this tablet. Save a backup after every session so nothing is lost.

How to use it

  1. Tap New match and give it a name.
  2. Tap Load video 1 and pick the video from this tablet.
  3. Press Play. Pause just after each ball finishes.
  4. On the Tag tab: type bowler and batter, tap the pitch where it landed, tap the field where it went, tap the runs, then Save ball.
  5. On the Balls tab: tap any ball to watch it again, or filter and press Play these clips to watch them one after another.
  6. On the Charts tab: see the pitch map, wagon wheel and numbers, and save a backup.

Looking closely

Use the Frame buttons to move one frame at a time, Speed for slow motion, and Zoom to enlarge. With Zoom on, choose Move and drag the picture. Choose Line, Arrow, Circle or Pen and draw on the video with your finger.

Good to know

The video is never uploaded. When you reopen the app, choose the same video again and all your tagged balls will still jump to the right moment. The pitch map is drawn from the bowler's end, so for a right-hander the off side is on the left.
